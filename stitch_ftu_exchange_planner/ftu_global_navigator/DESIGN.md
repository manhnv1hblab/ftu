## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an energetic, warm, and playful yet academic environment tailored specifically for Foreign Trade University (FTU) students planning their global exchange semesters.

The visual style blends modern university heritage with a spirited, Gen Z illustration-driven aesthetic. It employs tactile card surfaces, soft floating illustrations, playful micro-stickers, and expressive badges to demystify complex academic exchange guidelines. The atmosphere is encouraging and empowering rather than bureaucratic, removing intimidation around credit conversion matrices, language requirements, and application deadlines.

Crucially, the system balances playful charm with institutional credibility, maintaining clear, persistent advisory badges and disclaimer banners ensuring students recognize the advisory nature of the platform alongside official university approvals.

## Colors
The palette balances FTU's prestigious academic heritage with youthful vibrancy:

- **Primary (`#BE123C` / `#E11D48`)**: Inspired by the traditional FTU crimson red. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key conversion progress meters, and important milestones.
- **Secondary (`#1E3A8A` / `#1E293B`)**: Institutional navy representing global reach, trust, and deep academic rigor. Serves as high-contrast heading text, major navigational bars, and structured data tables.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` / `#D1FAE5`)**: Fresh mint accentuating credit conversion approval ratios, success confirmations, and active status tags.
- **Accent Yellow (`#FEF08A` / `#F59E0B`)**: Warm pastel sunshine used for highlighting tips, scholarship tags, mascot speech bubbles, and partner university spotlight banners.
- **Neutral Pearl Ground (`#F8FAFC`)**: Soft, clean background base preventing visual fatigue while accentuating warm card elevations and layered borders.
- **Disclaimer Callout Tone (`#FEF3C7` / `#92400E`)**: Dedicated gentle amber tone for the required platform advisory notifications.

## Typography
The typographic pairing emphasizes flawless Vietnamese diacritics alongside an approachable, lively personality:

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Contemporary, rounded terminals and open apertures inject youthful vigor and confidence into titles, statistics, and university program headers.
- **Body Text (Be Vietnam Pro)**: Tailored specifically for optimal Vietnamese legibility. It resolves heavy tone mark stacking with clarity across complex course syllabi, prerequisite descriptions, and transfer equivalencies.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Crisp geometric weights make credit tallies, country badges, deadlines, and validation chips glanceable and sharp.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column grid system structures wide desktop viewports (max-width `1280px`), adapting into 8 columns on tablets and 4 columns on mobile screens. Generous vertical breathing room supports the friendly, uncluttered personality.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column grid with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` column gutters. Multi-column split views display interactive course mapping planners side-by-side with partner university profiles.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` column gutters. Search filters shift into expandable top sheets.
- **Mobile (<= 767px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margins. Data-heavy credit comparison cards stack into stepped timeline blocks.
- **Sticky Academic Disclaimer**: Anchored to either top navigation or fixed footer banner on mobile, preserving transparent academic compliance without obscuring interactive actions.

## Elevation & Depth
The design system avoids stark, clinical drop shadows in favor of tinted, friendly ambient elevations:

- **Layer 0 (Base)**: Pure pearlescent ground (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Containers)**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate border (`1.5px solid #E2E8F0`) and a soft, warm-tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.06)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Mascot Callouts)**: Elevated white surfaces with `0 14px 32px -4px rgba(190, 18, 60, 0.08)`, accentuated by a gentle 1px border.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Study Planner Summary)**: Deep soft diffusion: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)`.
- **Hover Micro-lift**: Actionable cards shift `-3px` upward on the Y-axis with a subtle crimson or navy glow, signaling clickability.

## Shapes
A deeply rounded, organic shape language captures the playful Gen Z spirit. Standard cards feature generous `rounded-2xl` (1.25rem / 20px) to `rounded-3xl` (1.75rem / 28px) geometry. Chips, badges, and primary action buttons adopt pill silhouettes (`rounded-full`) to maintain an inviting, tactile rhythm throughout the experience.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Pill-shaped with FTU Crimson background (`#BE123C`), white text, bold font weight, accompanied by a subtle inset top highlight. Hover transitions smoothly to `#E11D48`.
- **Secondary**: Pill-shaped with soft translucent Navy tint (`#EEF2FF`), `#1E3A8A` text, and a crisp 1.5px border (`#C7D2FE`).
- **Tertiary / Utility**: Ghost pill button with subtle color hover fills and micro icon pairings.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags (e.g., Equivalent Approved, Pending Review)**: Mint pill (`#D1FAE5`) with dark green text (`#065F46`), or Amber pill (`#FEF3C7`) with warm gold text (`#92400E`).
- **Credit Value Tags**: Compact pill badges displaying FTU ECTS/Credits conversions with soft background tints.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Rounded pill text fields (`rounded-full` or `rounded-2xl` for text areas) with `#F1F5F9` background, `#1E293B` text, and a soft focus state featuring a 2px crimson outline (`#BE123C`) and faint ambient ring. Mascot search tips float dynamically next to active input boxes.

### University & Subject Equivalency Cards
- Thick, pill-friendly cards with pure white fill, 1.5px slate border (`#E2E8F0`), and soft crimson-navy ambient shadows.
- Visual hierarchy separates partner university logo, global ranking badge, semester deadlines, and transferable FTU credit counts into compartmentalized, rounded sub-containers.

### Study Planner & Credit Mapping Drawer
- Interactive drag-and-drop course module boxes with dashed playful borders (`2px dashed #CBD5E1`) when unmapped, converting to solid mint surfaces (`#ECFDF5`) when an FTU equivalent syllabus is mapped.

### Official Disclaimer Bar (Mandatory)
- Permanent, rounded banner styled with warm pastel yellow (`#FEF3C7`), an amber information badge icon, and clear copy in `body-sm`: *"Lưu ý: Nền tảng chỉ hỗ trợ tra cứu và lập kế hoạch tham khảo. Kết quả xét duyệt tín chỉ chính thức do Phòng Quản lý Đào tạo FTU quyết định."*
